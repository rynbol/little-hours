## Graphics loop

The owner judged the look "Fortnite with simple graphics" and asked for a loop toward the Breath of the Wild bar. Each round changes one look mechanism and shoots the same views before and after. A fresh blind judge then sees each pair in random order next to the four reference stills, picks the side closer to the bar and scores both from 1 to 10. A round is kept only if it wins blind and the frame checks still pass. Winning a pair is not the goal. The owner set the bar at an absolute 8.5 out of 10, so each round also logs the mean score of the after shots, and the loop runs until that mean reaches 8.5. Absolute scores drift between judges: round 1's after shots scored 4.0 from one judge and 3.0 from the next, so only a mean judged in the same round counts.

## What looks or plays wrong

- The far ranges are streaky and washed out. Their banding reads as stretched texture, not rock.
- Flowers are small at the whole-valley scale and only read up close.
- The waterfall recess is an open crevice rather than a cave behind the water.
- Morning at 6.8 is a dull brown dawn. Morning shots use 8.5.
- At gallop and dash speed a planted front paw dips up to 4 cm below the ground between keyframes. The dash moves 18 cm a frame, so the dip shows for one frame.
- At the end of a gallop or dash stride the front paws float short of the ground: 1.4 cm for the cat, up to 3 cm for the bunny and panda. The bunny's short front legs float 4.4 cm in the sit and 4.3 cm while knocked out.
- The fox and wolf ruffs are a little ridged, and the bodies read slightly long and tubular from the side.
- Each pet file is 1.4 to 1.7 MB because every clip keys every joint at 60 fps. The wolf is loaded on every visit, even before it is earned.
- The bond ribbon in Blender's preview is a muddy honey colour. In the game it takes the bond level's colour.
- When the defeat camera falls back it can end up inside the stag's body.
- Keys are baked at 30 fps, so on the first frame of a fast strike the sword can trail the sim's blade by up to 25 cm. The mantle's hand misses its ledge by 42 cm.
- The strafe crosses the legs at some speeds. The wrists bend sharply in some holds.
- The parry flick cuts in even mid-roll. Petting plays only when the hero stands close enough to the pet.
- The scarf reads as a pillow, the boots are rounded, and the cardigan looks like leather under flat light. The sword is long for a short sword.
- In the roll the bun dips 3 cm under the floor, and lying down the cape dips 1.7 cm.
- The hero file is 5.96 MB because every clip keys every joint. Dropping constant tracks would shrink it.
- In the close camera at morning the hero's face is darker than in Blender.
- The stag's head is still a little horse-like. Its mane is one even strip of moss.
- Lying down in the rest and defeat poses, the stag's legs fold into a puddle under the body instead of tucking cleanly.
- The stag file is 2.4 MB because every clip keys every joint's scale. Dropping the constant tracks would roughly halve it.
- From outside the ring the stag reads small, and the camera often loses it behind a standing stone during the fight.
- The camera pulls in hard when a standing stone is behind you, so a stone or the capsule can fill the frame.
- From the ring the wolf is a small dark shape on the cliff top. It needs a rim of light or a howl to be noticed.
- The antler trophy is thin for the room's scale, and its glow is faint at the room's zoom.
