# Style round 1

Two fresh reviewers saw only six anonymous A/B pairs each. Camera angles and day, dusk, and rain settings matched for scenery. Character pairs used three avatar angles, two cat angles, and the Warden against the existing puppy as a creature-finish reference.

Scenery passed. Wilds was weaker in 2 of 6 pairs. Judgments were B, B, tie, tie, B, B. Wilds occupied B, B, B, A, A, A. The named difference was sharper shadow bands and flatter foreground foliage in the weaker pictures. Both rain pairs tied. Forest geometry and atmosphere remain shared.

Characters failed. Wilds was weaker in 5 of 6 pairs. Judgments were B, A, A, B, B, B. Wilds occupied B, A, A, B, B, A.

- Cat front. Heavy eye rings crowd the face. Flat muzzle and paws, angular shoulders.
- Cat three-quarter. Abrupt shoulder planes, slablike legs, jagged fur patches.
- Avatar three-quarter. Dark uniform shading hides facial and clothing volume. Raised shoulders look stiff.
- Avatar side. Broad hair, neck, and back facets. Angular knees and narrow wrists.
- Avatar front. Flat face and cardigan. Shoulder corners and abrupt wrists.
- Warden comparison. Wilds won. Its continuous anatomy, hooves, antlers, and foliage were stronger than the reference puppy's separated leg shapes.

Next revision softens avatar and cat anatomy and adds broad indirect light to preserve form in the shadowed faces. The Warden and shared scenery stay as reviewed. The next character round uses a fresh reviewer.
