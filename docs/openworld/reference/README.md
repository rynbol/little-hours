# Provisional visual targets

These four stills are generated concept references. They are not screenshots of the build. Owner approval is pending.

- `player-day.png` shows adult proportions, the hooded Ranger, a readable sword and lifted forest shadows.
- `sword-fight-dusk.png` shows the intended visibility of the hand, blade and target in warm evening light. It does not expand this goal into Warden or pet art.
- `running-grass-rain.png` shows fine curved grass as a continuous field and readable overcast lighting.
- `wide-vista.png` shows the intended open horizon, depth and atmosphere. It does not add new biomes or locations to this goal.

The built-in image-generation tool made the stills on 2026-10-02. `prompts.json` preserves the exact prompts. The day image was the identity reference for the other three.

The character starts from the supplied Quaternius Female Ranger. Its hood, layered tunic and broad bracers give a clear silhouette at third-person distance. The runtime asset will use the pack's geometry and rig, vertex colour gradients and a simple original sword.

These pictures have more flowers, surface detail and scenery variety than the intended runtime slice. The build must keep flowers rare and follow the explicit asset, scope and performance requirements. The rain image is the clearest target for grass density and colour continuity.
