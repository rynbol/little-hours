# The Wilds target

The owner rejected the previous style frame. Its reviews and images have been removed.

The new target is adult proportions, painterly forest lighting, broad colour fields and airy distance. Breath of the Wild informs atmosphere and grass. Palworld informs the partner combat concept. Characters, locations and symbols remain original.

The four target stills under `reference/` are provisional until the owner approves them. Gameplay character bodies and motion come from the supplied Quaternius CC0 packs, edited in Blender. The player uses vertex colours, StandardMaterial and PainterlyPlugin. No textures, normal maps or specular highlights are planned.

Every player and grass review uses shuffled pairs against the previous build in day, dusk and rain, with the reference alongside. A pass wins every pair and scores at least eight in each light. Stop after four rounds per piece and record the actual scores, including failures.
