# Web, social, and direct 3D focus competitors

Research date: September 21, 2026. This is primary-source desk research, not a hands-on product evaluation. Features below are official product or developer/store claims. “Available” means the official page currently offers access or purchase, not that every feature was tested. The list is a broad competitive scan, not a claim of exhaustiveness. Tiny indie listings and prototypes are separated from established products; store presence does not establish traction.

## Benchmark decision

**gogh: Focus with Your Avatar is the closest overall functional benchmark found.** It already combines customizable 3D rooms, avatars, productivity tools, an evolving companion, multiplayer, and a desktop mini-window. A proposed “3D room + focus + pets + friends + small desktop view” is therefore not an unoccupied combination. Its [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3213850/gogh_Focus_with_Your_Avatar/) establishes these features.

**lofi.town is the strongest browser-specific reference** for personal space, identity, focus and friends. **On-Together** is an equally important reference for keeping social presence at the edge of the desktop. **Flocus** is a useful benchmark for focused, compact productivity controls. These selections are product judgments based on documented features, not measured customer preference.

The strongest proposed difference to validate is one persistent home and companion across an instant browser session and native desktop/notch presence, with private friend sessions that are easy to join. This is a narrower positioning hypothesis than merely adding a pet, multiplayer or mini mode. There is no verified claim here that every competitor lacks this combination.

## gogh: definitive comparison evidence

The [current Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3213850/gogh_Focus_with_Your_Avatar/) confirms room/character customization, productivity tools, the evolving Kuroda 42 companion, multiplayer, and desktop Mini Window Mode. Windows and Apple Silicon macOS requirements are listed. No browser edition was found in the reviewed official sources; say **“no browser edition found”**, not “browser is technically impossible.”

The mobile editions are separately listed for [iPhone/iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gogh-focus-with-your-avatar/id6478642737) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_CA&id=com.goghjapan.gogh_android). They document customizable 3D rooms/avatars, music, timers and shared Spaces. Do not project every Steam feature onto mobile.

The developer's [February 17, 2026 explanation](https://gogh.gg/news/en/20260217) says Steam and Mobile are separate applications with different development teams and architectures. It discusses users' account-linking requests, device performance, and why mobile shared Spaces differ from editable desktop multiplayer Rooms. It is historical explanatory evidence, not the latest feature list.

The [official comparison updated July 30, 2026](https://gogh.zendesk.com/hc/ja/articles/55533138384793-Key-Differences-Between-the-Mobile-Version-and-the-STEAM-Version) is the better source for current edition differences. It lists Steam room publication, Workshop, text/drawing chat, habits/journal and companion growth. Mobile instead has shared Spaces, stickers and an in-app friends system. This demonstrates platform divergence; it does not prove every possible future synchronization feature is absent.

The [multiplayer help page updated July 31, 2026](https://gogh.zendesk.com/hc/ja/articles/55524515648025-Multiplayer) adds useful interaction detail: guests see the host's Kuroda 42; environment settings initialize from the host then can differ locally; Focus Mode hides emotes and join/leave notices; rooms have lifecycle/hosting constraints. It also documents blocking, kicking and reporting. **Older descriptions claiming “no chat in multiplayer” are stale** relative to the July 30 feature comparison.

## What to borrow and what to validate

1. Use gogh as the broad functional benchmark for room, avatar, companion and focus integration. The final product choice in the companion competitor map is Virtual Cottage 2, because the user specifically wants Rooms-style whole-room framing. Compare actual visual quality and session friction in a later hands-on review before claiming ours is better.
2. Borrow lofi.town's quick browser entry and personal-space/social loop; keep the initial product useful alone.
3. Use On-Together's desktop presence as evidence that the room need not occupy the screen during real work. A notch is a specific native surface, not a new category by itself.
4. Validate cross-surface continuity: same room, same companion, same active timer, and lightweight private friend presence without rebuilding identity on separate apps. gogh's documented platform divergence is a concrete starting hypothesis.
5. Test whether people return because the space feels personal and helps them start work. Avoid treating furniture counts, a timer or generic AI chat as sufficient differentiation.

No performance, retention, accessibility, privacy or willingness-to-pay claims were measured in this research. Audience/review totals were intentionally not used as comparable market-size estimates.
