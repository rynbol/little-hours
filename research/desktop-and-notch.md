# Desktop, notch and coding companion competitors

Research checked September 21, 2026 (Pacific time). This is a broad, targeted competitor map, not a claim that every small indie project has been found. Features below are supported by the linked developer, publisher, repository or official store sources; products were not installed or functionally tested.

**Mac Pet is the clearest verified study-timer-plus-notch-pet competitor. FlowNotch claims an even closer pet-room-plus-focus-plus-notch combination, but its public materials are inconsistent.** A stronger product hypothesis is one persistent, beautiful room that follows the user between a full room, a small desktop window and a compact notch companion. That is a hypothesis to test, not a claim of an uncontested market.

## FlowNotch: stronger than a landing page, weaker than independently tested parity

The [official download page](https://flownotch.com/download) links to a [versioned v1.2.2 DMG](https://updates.flownotch.com/releases/1.2.2/flownotch-1.2.2.dmg). A read-only HTTP HEAD request returned HTTP 200, content type `application/x-apple-diskimage`, length 15,740,696 bytes and last-modified July 11, 2026. The [changelog](https://flownotch.com/changelog) lists that release. This establishes a publicly hosted download, not tested functionality or verified code signing.

The [homepage](https://flownotch.com/) says technical preview, v0.4 and free for personal use. Other pages describe a paid trial and v1.x releases, and usage figures conflict. Recommended wording: **Downloadable early product; claims pet rooms, pixel scenes and focus-reactive moods; functionality not independently tested.** Avoid using its usage numbers as market evidence.

Do not label it dead based on forum posts or infer pet features from other products with similar names.

## Technical implications

1. **Draw beside or below the physical camera housing.** Apple documents the visible top-corner regions with [`NSScreen.auxiliaryTopLeftArea`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsscreen/auxiliarytopleftarea-uglc), `auxiliaryTopRightArea` and safe-area APIs. A room inside the black hardware notch is a visual illusion, not usable display pixels.
2. **A browser concept and a native desktop presence are different deliverables.** A webpage can demonstrate a compact-room mode. For persistent presence over other apps, the implementation recommendation is a separate desktop shell. [DeskCat](https://github.com/ppxinyue/DeskCat) documents transparent frameless windows/native panel handling; [Atoll](https://github.com/rutmehta/Atoll) documents an NSPanel host and display-dependent geometry.
3. **Avoid assuming all notch features are necessary.** Atoll's own media/HUD implementation uses private/system techniques and cannot be sandboxed, according to its README. That constraint applies to those choices; it does not establish that a simple pet/timer overlay must use private APIs or cannot ship through the App Store. [Notch Pet's App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notch-pet/id6455990267?mt=12) demonstrates a narrower companion route.
4. **Use the same identity at three scales.** Suggested product direction: full 3D room → small room window → pet/timer beside the notch. This is our design recommendation, not a sourced competitor capability.

## What this changes about positioning

Notch utilities, Pomodoro pets, AI-agent pets and work-driven pet growth all have existing competitors. The useful hypothesis to test is continuity and emotional attachment: a room that feels like the user's place, supports actual focus, stays present at different sizes, and later welcomes a few friends. A feature checklist alone will not distinguish it.
