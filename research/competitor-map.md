# Little Hours — competitor map and product direction

**Research date: September 21, 2026. Working product name: Little Hours.**

The concept is a cozy, whole-room **cutaway isometric dollhouse** that people keep open while studying or working. The room should be readable as a small object, with a desk, shelves, light, weather and a companion. The agreed sequence is **browser first, then desktop/notch, then social**.

**Choose Virtual Cottage 2 as the overall product benchmark. Use Rooms as the primary art and interaction reference, gogh as the strongest broad functionality rival, and Spirit City as a reference for focus rituals and attachment.** This is a product judgment based on the requested whole-room framing, not a numeric ranking or evidence that one product is objectively best. [Virtual Cottage 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2943180/Virtual_Cottage_2/) combines an intimate customizable home, pets, focus tools and shared spaces; [Rooms](https://rooms.xyz/) is the reference for the room itself as an inviting, interactive miniature.

The landscape already contains room decoration, timers, companions, multiplayer and small desktop modes. The opportunity to test is a particularly good combination: immediate browser access, a beautiful diorama that remains useful while work happens, and eventually one continuous room and companion across surfaces. We should demonstrate that advantage rather than claim an empty category.

## Evidence and scope

This scan covers more than 50 distinct products across cozy rooms, focus tools, virtual coworking, desktop pets and notch utilities. It is deliberately broad; it cannot establish that every indie launch or prototype has been found. StudyTogether and StudyStream are treated as one current ecosystem, and a renamed AgentPet fork is not counted as an independent concept.

Facts come from official product pages, developer documentation, source repositories and publisher-operated store listings. Rooms received visual browser inspection; the other products were researched from their primary pages rather than installed and tested. A listed feature is a developer claim, not a measured assessment of quality. Release status is recorded as observed on the research date. Regional prices and temporary promotions are not used to rank products.

Detailed evidence is retained in [web and social research](./web-and-social.md) and [desktop and notch research](./desktop-and-notch.md). The former identifies gogh as the closest functional bundle; the decision here additionally weights the user's whole-room dollhouse direction, making Virtual Cottage 2 the overall benchmark.

## Uncertainties worth carrying forward

- **FlowNotch is downloadable, but feature claims need testing.** Its official [download page](https://flownotch.com/download) routes to a versioned DMG that returned HTTP 200 to a read-only header check; its [changelog](https://flownotch.com/changelog) lists v1.2.2. Its homepage and other pages disagree on version, pricing model and usage figures. Treat rooms/pets/focus as developer claims; do not infer verified 3D or use the usage figures as market evidence.
- **NotchNook could not be freshly verified.** The [official site](https://lo.cafe/notchnook) repeatedly timed out. Current availability, pricing and alleged pet features remain uncertain. A timeout does not establish discontinuation.
- **gogh editions differ.** The developer's [July 2026 comparison](https://gogh.zendesk.com/hc/ja/articles/55533138384793-Key-Differences-Between-the-Mobile-Version-and-the-STEAM-Version) documents distinct Steam and mobile capabilities. No browser edition was found in the reviewed official sources. That is scoped research evidence, not proof about future releases.
- **Roadmaps stay separate from shipping features.** Study Buddies, StudyHub, DeskPet and the Mac edition of Mini Cozy Room need their specific prototype, roadmap or playtest caveats retained. Their linked listings above are the evidence.
- **A notch is a physical obstruction.** Apple's [screen-area API documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsscreen/auxiliarytopleftarea-uglc) describes usable space beside the camera housing. A real companion would draw beside/below it. An in-browser compact mode is a design demonstration, not native notch integration.

## The product hypothesis

**Little Hours is your tiny room for getting things done: open a link, settle in, and let the same room and companion stay with you.**

Five proposed advantages should guide the implementation and later be tested:

1. **Immediate browser access.** Start alone without an installation or account requirement. This is an advantage to validate against downloaded room products, not a claim that no browser competitors exist.
2. **Diorama first.** Keep the whole room legible. Warm lighting, restrained motion and carefully composed objects should make one small space worth returning to before a large asset catalog is built.
3. **One identity across sizes.** Persist the same room, companion and active focus session. Later desktop and notch modes should feel like different views of that place, not separate pets to set up again.
4. **Performance as a feature.** The room exists while other work runs. Render only what is useful, provide reduced motion, offer a compact/static option, and measure resource use on a named baseline device.
5. **Quiet, opt-in friends.** Later, let a few invited friends show up with shared focus state and small reactions. Presence should help work begin; communication and public discovery can wait until the solo experience retains users.

These are design and positioning hypotheses. No retention, battery, productivity, accessibility or willingness-to-pay advantage has been established by this competitor research.

## MVP and staged plan

| Stage | Deliverable | Evidence required before expanding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Browser room** | One original procedural cutaway room and companion; a small set of lighting/weather choices and object interactions; focus timer, simple tasks, local persistence and a compact in-page view. Clear start/pause/resume/end behavior. | People understand it immediately, finish work sessions and choose to reopen it. Verify browser refresh, sleep/background tabs, expired-session restore and reduced motion. |
| **2. Personal home** | A deliberately small decoration set, saved layouts and progress that rewards completed focus. Optional account-based sync only when needed. | Users care about their own room and return for the room/work loop, rather than only browsing settings once. |
| **3. Desktop and notch** | Native desktop companion with the same room/pet identity and timer state; compact room window and a companion/timer beside the notch. Support external or notchless displays. | Stable behavior across displays/spaces, low measured background cost and reliable state handoff. The browser prototype does not prove these. |
| **4. Quiet social** | Invite-only rooms for a few friends, shared focus state, optional synchronized timers and lightweight reactions; clear leave/mute controls. | Friends can join easily and report that presence helps focus without increasing interruptions. Measure solo retention separately. |
| **5. Optional agent presence** | A small opt-in companion response to coding-agent working/waiting/done events, if the target users actually want it. | The agent surface improves useful awareness without making the room dependent on coding tools or token consumption. |

**Current build status:** the first local browser prototype is being developed. Its assets are original procedural assets. This dossier does not claim that native notch integration, cross-device sync, multiplayer or agent hooks have shipped. The room and visual interaction pattern are references to study; no competitor assets or proprietary implementation are being copied into the project.

## Proposed validation gates

The following are initial targets for an instrumented pilot, **not competitor data, industry benchmarks or achieved results**. Revisit them after the first 20 target-user sessions.

| Question | Proposed test / initial target |
| --- | --- |
| Can people begin without instruction? | At least 16 of 20 first-time participants start a focus session within 30 seconds of the room becoming interactive. Observe where others hesitate. |
| Does it survive real work? | At least 14 of 20 participants complete one planned 20–30 minute session; record why sessions end early rather than counting only clicks. |
| Is the place worth reopening? | At least 10 of 20 return on three separate days during a one-week pilot without a personal reminder from the builder. Treat this small sample as directional. |
| Is the room pleasant rather than distracting? | At least 16 of 20 rate visual distraction at 2 or lower on a five-point scale after working. Compare full-room and compact modes. |
| Is loading friction acceptable? | Aim for p75 interactive time below 3 seconds on a declared baseline such as M1 MacBook Air, current Chromium and a controlled 30 Mbps connection. Record cold/warm loads separately. |
| Is continuous rendering appropriate? | Target stable 60 fps in the default active room on that baseline, a lower-power compact option, and no scheduled animation loop while the tab is hidden. Measure CPU/GPU/battery impact rather than claiming it is lightweight. |
| Is session state reliable? | All defined refresh/pause/resume/background/clock-expiry cases preserve the intended remaining time and credit a completed session once. |

If users admire the room but do not return to work in it, improve the start-work ritual before adding social infrastructure. If they return but leave the room hidden, compact presence and performance deserve priority over more furniture.

## Business model to test

Start with a useful **free core**: one complete room, a companion, dependable focus tools and basic personalization. Test paid cosmetic collections, additional room themes or optional supporter benefits after usage is established. These are hypotheses, not a final price or evidence that customers will pay. [lofi.town](https://www.lofi.town/) demonstrates a free-core/supporter pattern; [Desktop Mate](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3301060/Desktop_Mate/) demonstrates a free base with paid character content. Their existence does not establish the right economics for this product.

The immediate goal is to make a small room people want beside them while doing real work. Virtual Cottage 2 is the best overall reference for that loop under the agreed framing; Rooms sets the visual ambition, and gogh defines how much existing functionality we must take seriously.
